# Code of conduct

Collaborate with technical rigor and respect. Discuss evidence and trade-offs,
protect credentials and plant/target information, disclose uncertainty and keep
reviews focused. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate
disruption, sexualized conduct and publication of private information are not
acceptable.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation. Report conduct
concerns privately; do not expose identifying information in public issues.
